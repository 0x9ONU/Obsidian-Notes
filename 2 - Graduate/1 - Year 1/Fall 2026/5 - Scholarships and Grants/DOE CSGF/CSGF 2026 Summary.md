# Basic Information

- $45,000 annual stipend
- Full tuition & Required fees
- An annual academic allowance
- Renews every 4 years
- A three-month practicum at one of 22 DOE laboratories
- Made for Ph.D. students at accredited U.S. university
- Must be a U.S. citizens

# Track 1: Interdisiplinary Science and Engineering

- Can be for almost any field
- Must contain research that applies “high-performance computing (HPC) to complex research problems”

# Track 2: Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or Computational Science

- Research interests that help scientists use emerging HPC systems more effectively
- Includes focus on issues in HPC as a broad technology as well as a particular applications
- *This is what I will be applying for*
