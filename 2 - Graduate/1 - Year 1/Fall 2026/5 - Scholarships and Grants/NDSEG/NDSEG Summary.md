
# Basic Information

- ~$43,200 annual stipend (check current cycle for exact figure)
- Full tuition & mandatory fees covered (not room/board)
- Health insurance contribution (~$1,600/yr)
- Up to $5,000 to attend the DoD National Fellows Conference (required, 2nd year)
- 3-year fellowship (36 months tenure)
- Sponsored jointly by AFOSR (Air Force), ARO (Army), and ONR (Navy)
- Must be U.S. citizen or U.S. national — permanent residents are NOT eligible (unlike Hertz)

# Eligibility

- Must be within first two years of graduate study, with at least three years of doctoral study remaining at time of application
- Applies to 15 supported STEM disciplines, including cognitive/neural/behavioral sciences
- Research must align with one or more DoD Broad Agency Announcement (BAA) topics — this is the key differentiator from DOE CSGF/Hertz
- Open to any nonprofit U.S. institution offering a Ph.D. in science or engineering

# Breakdown of Submission

1. **Application Form**: Contact info, demographics, field of study, proposed research and its relevance to DoD
2. **Proposed DoD Sponsoring Agency**: Pick one of AFOSR/ARO/ONR + a research technical discipline area — any agency can choose to fund your application regardless of which you pick
3. **Research Proposal/Essay**: Needs to explicitly tie into DoD strategy documents / BAAs, not just general HPC or science framing
4. **Personal Statement**
5. **Three (3) Reference Evaluation Forms**: Submitted directly by recommenders through the portal (not just letters — a structured evaluation form)
6. **Official Transcripts**

# Notes

- Unlike DOE CSGF/Hertz, DoD alignment is explicit and load-bearing — read the current BAAs and DoD Strategy Document before drafting your research essay, and consider citing them
- Application is only considered complete once ALL sections (including all three references) are submitted — nothing is accepted after the deadline, so don't leave recommenders for the last week
- Recommended early timeline (from univ. fellowship offices): first personal statement draft ~mid-September, first research essay draft ~late September, second drafts ~mid-to-late October, ahead of the Oct 30 deadline
