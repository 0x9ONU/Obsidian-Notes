# Basic Information

- Up to $250,000 total value over the fellowship
- $45,000 annual stipend + full tuition/fees (paid to school)
- Up to 5 years of funding
- No service obligation, but expects a "duty year" commitment (to work on tech relevant to national security if called upon)
- Lifelong mentorship & Hertz Fellows community (1,300+ fellows) 
- For Ph.D. study at one of ~40 approved participating schools
- Applicants must be U.S. citizens or permanent residents

# Eligibility

- Open to applied physical, biological, and engineering sciences and mathematics — NOT for research in medicine, biology aimed at animal/human treatment, or social sciences
- Must have (or be about to have) a bachelor's degree, and not yet be too far into a Ph.D. program
- Undergrad seniors, gap-year applicants, and current grad students (early in program) are all eligible

# Breakdown of Submission

1. **Application Form**: Standard biographical/academic info
2. **Essays**: Personal statement + research-focused essays (exact prompts released with portal — check current cycle)
3. **Official Transcripts**
4. **Recommendation Letters**: Multiple recommenders, submitted directly by them
5. **Interviews (if selected)**: Two rounds — Round 1 (technical, by fellow/scientist volunteers) then Round 2 (more rigorous, in-person or video) for finalists

# Notes

- Much more interview-heavy than DOE CSGF — technical interviews are a hallmark of Hertz and are known to be tough/wide-ranging across your field
- Fellowship targets research "freedom" — funding isn't tied to a specific proposed project the way some fellowships are
- Notable past fellows include Dario Amodei and Jared Kaplan (Anthropic co-founders)
