## Essay

My major academic gap period came from May 2026 to Aug 2026. Between finishing my undergraduate degree and starting my PhD at the University of Cincinnati, I contributed to my last undergraduate research project. During this time, I took the initiative and began advising a student who was iterating on my previous work. I met with him often to review his test results, interpret trends in the data, and co-author and edit the reports that eventually turned into a conference paper. We went on to publish a paper at MWSCAS 2026 on ARQ Trojan attacks in energy-harvesting networks [1].  

Other independent activities I participated in are as follows:
- Theta Chi Fraternity, Member (Oct 2022 - May 2026), Vice President (Dec 2023 - Dec 2024), President (Dec 2024 - Dec 2025): I coordinated the chapter to meet university and international standards, provided over 100 service hours to the community, and raised over $6000 for charity by organizing a yearly rock concert called Rock the Tundra. I selected and scheduled the bands while handling the live mixing for crowds up to 100 of spectators. My time in the fraternity taught me how to manage multiple teams and mentor future students.
- Tau Beta Pi, Member (Oct 2024 - May 2026), Historian (Nov 2024 - Nov 2025): as Historian of the engineering honor society, I was tasked with archiving the history of the organization at the Ohio Iota chapter. I helped lead the new member process for 13 initiates, including the Bent polishing tradition (the society’s trestle emblem) and the initiation ceremony. Additionally, I helped co-lead weekly walk-in tutoring sessions for the whole college. As the Historian, I also learned how to keep detailed records on a timeline, a skill that directly supports tracking research progress and communicating it to an advisor. 
- Tabletop Club, Community Event Organizer (December 2024 - Present): I host and lead team-based weekly events, where I manage tabletop sessions as a game master for around a dozen participants at a time. This role requires clear communication, creative problem-solving, and strict time management.
