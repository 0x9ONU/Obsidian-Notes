
# DataBridge

- 8 proposals funded by the NSF
- A social network for data
- A lot of universities have gained hundreds of years worth of social-science data
- Take the concepts of social media and implement it as a data network
- There is an enormous amount of dark data (aka. data that is not processed yet)
- **Goal**: Shining a light on dark data. Maximize the usefulness of long tail data for scientific research
- Assist scientists in discovering “interesting” data sets by automatically forming *communities of data*
