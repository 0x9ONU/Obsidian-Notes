[[Security in Energy Harvesting Networks A Survey on Current Solutions and Research Challenges.pdf]]  [1] is a survey paper published by Pietro Tedeschi, Savio Sciancalepore, and Roberto Di Pietro that contains various methods to both attack and defend energy-harvesting wireless sensor networks (EH-WSN). After previewing the nearly 250 papers that were cited in the article, there were 15 papers that most closely matched our research contributions. Two of the papers [2], [3] focused on the potential eavesdropping attacks such that a passive attacker can intercept signals between sensor nodes and their base station.
