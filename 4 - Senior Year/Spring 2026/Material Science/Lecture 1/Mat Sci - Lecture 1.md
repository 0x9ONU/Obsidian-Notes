## Classification of Materials

```ad-important
Broken down into **three classical categories**:
1. Metals
2. Ceramics
3. Polymers
```

Any combination of these classical categories are called *composites*.

### Metal

Composed of one or more **metallic** elements
- Many combined with nonmetallic elements

*Properties*:
- Stiff
- Strong
- Ductile
- High thermal conductivity
- High electrical conductivity
- Opaque reflective

### Ceramics

Compounds of metallic and nonmetallic elements

*Properties*:
- Brittle
- Glassy
- Elastic
- Insulators (non-conductors)

```ad-example
- Aluminum Oxide - $Al_2O_3$
- Silicon Carbide - $SiC$
- Iron Oxides - 
	- Ferrous Oxide (Wursite) $FeO$
	- Ferric Oxide (Hematite) $Fe_2O_3$
	- Magnettite: $Fe_3O_4$
```
### Polymers

Very long molecular structures
- Plastic and rubber materials

*Properties*
- Soft
- Ductile
- Low strength
- Low density
- Thermal and electrical insulators

```ad-example
- PC - polycarbonate
- PVC - polyvinyl chloride
```

### Composites

Composed of two or more individual materials

```ad-example
- Concree
- Camboo
- Wood
- Bone tissues
```

### Use Case: Biomimicry

Finding something in nature and engineering a design that “mimics” it

```ad-example
- Geckso can run up vertical walls and horizontally because they create weak chemical bonds across the material they are on.
- Some plants have *hydrophobic surfaces* that resist water since the material refused to bond with water.
```

## Chemical Bonding

### Primary

**Ionic**: Non-directional; valance electrons transfer; *ceramics*

![[Pasted image 20260120123747.png]]

**Covalent**: Directional; sharing of electrons; *ceramics AND polymers*

![[Pasted image 20260120123755.png]]

**Metallic**: Nondirectional; *metals*
- An array of positive ions surrounded by a sea of mobile valence electrons
- Allows for electrons to flow freely through the structure (electrically conductive)

![[Pasted image 20260120123801.png]]

### Secondary

**Hydrogen Bonding**: A hydrogen atom located between a pair of other atoms having a high affinity fo electrons

![[Pasted image 20260120123941.png]]

**Van der Waals Bonding**: 
- Dipole - an uneven change distrivution occurs
- *Polymers* (between molecular chains)
