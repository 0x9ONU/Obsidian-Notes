# Question 1

```ad-question
Calculate the ASTM Grain Size Number for the following condition: Approximately 64 grains seen in a 1"x1" area at 100X mag. Please show all the steps of your work.
```

$$
N = \text{Grains} \times \left(\frac{N_{mag}}{N_{100}}\right)^2
$$
$$
N = 64 * \left( \frac{100}{100} \right)^2=64
$$
$$
N =2^{n-1}
$$
$$
(64) = 2^{n-1}
$$
$$
\log_{64}(4)=n-1
$$
$$
8 = n-1
$$
$$
\boxed{n=7}
$$
# Question 2

```ad-question
Calculate the ASTM Grain Size Number for the following condition: Approximately 16 grains seen in a 1"x1" area at 200X mag. Please show all the steps of your work.
```

$$
N = \text{Grains} \times \left (  \frac{N_{mag}}{N_{100}}  \right)^2
$$
$$
= 16 \times \left( \frac{200}{100} \right)^2
$$
$$
N = 64
$$
