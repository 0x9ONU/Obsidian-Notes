# Alternative Types of Stress

**Axial Stress**
![[Pasted image 20260219120632.png]]
$$
\sigma = \frac{F}{A_{o}}
$$
**Simple Shear Stress**

![[Pasted image 20260219120707.png]]

$$
\tau = \frac{P}{A}
$$

**Bending Stress**

![[Pasted image 20260219120712.png]]

$$
\sigma = \frac{M_{c}}{I}
$$

**Torsional Shear Stress**
![[Pasted image 20260219120717.png]]
$$
\tau = \frac{T_{\gamma}}{J}
$$

# Tensile Test

The tensile test tests *axial stress*
- We use the properties found from the axial laoding to evaluate other loading modes
- Axial Stress is the same across the volume if there is a constant cross-sectiobn
- Stress *varies by location*
