
## Strategy One: Reducing Grain Size

**Grain boundaries are barrier to slip**:
- A barrier “strength” increases with increasing angle of misorientation
- Smaller grain size → more barriers to slip

$$
\sigma_{yield}=\sigma_{o}+k_{y}d^{-1/2}
$$
- $d$ - Average Grain Diameter
