### Components and Phases

**Components**:
- The elemtns of compounds which are present in the ally
- (ex. Al and Cu)

**Phases**: 
- The chemically homogeneous and physically distinct material regions that form
- (ex. $\alpha$ and $\beta$)
- Happens because two elements will melt at different points, which form the regions based on how they heat and cool

## Solubility Limit

```ad-summary
A limit that how much solute can be dissolved into a solvent
```

- **For Solids**: Atoms of the solute *substitute* on the crystal lattcies of the solvent, or go into *interstices* between solvent atoms
- Greater the difference in size, and in electronegativity, the lower the solubility limit.

### Phase Rules

#### Rule 1: If we know $T$ and $C_{o}$, then we know which phases are present

![[Pasted image 20260331122909.png]]

#### Rule 2: If we know $T$ and $C_{o}$, then we can determine the composition of each phase

![[Pasted image 20260331122946.png]]

#### Rule 3: If we know $T$ and $C_{o}$, then we can determine the *weight fraction* of each phase

**Tie Line**: Connects the phases in equilibrium with each other
- Also called an **isotherm**

![[Pasted image 20260331123450.png]]

$$
W_{L} = \frac{M_{L}}{M_{L}+M_{\alpha}}=\frac{S}{R+S}= \frac{C_{\alpha}-C_{o}}{C_{\alpha}-C_{L}}
$$
$$
W_{\alpha}=\frac{R}{R+S}= \frac{C_{o}-C_{L}}{C_{\alpha}-C_{L}}
$$
