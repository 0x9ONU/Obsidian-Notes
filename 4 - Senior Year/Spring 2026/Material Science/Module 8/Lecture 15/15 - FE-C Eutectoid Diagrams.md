# Eutetic, Eutectoid, & Pertetic

**Eutetic**: Liquid transforms into two solid phases

$L \leftrightarrow \alpha + \beta$

**Eutectoid**: One solid phase transforms into two *other* solid phases

$S_{2} \leftrightarrow S_{1}+S_{3}$

(*for steel*)
$\gamma \leftrightarrow \alpha + Fe_{3}C$

**Peritectic**: Liquid and one solid pahse transform into a second solid phase

$S_{1} + L \leftrightarrow S_{2}$

*For Steel*

$\delta + L \leftrightarrow \gamma$

## Example Problem

```ad-question
For a 99.6 wt % Fe-0.40 wt% C steel at a temperature just below the eutctoid, determine the following:
1. The compositions of $Fe_3 C$ and ferrite ($\alpha$)
2. The amount of cementite (in grams) that forms in 100 g of steel
```

*Part 1*: Use the $RS$ tie line just below the eutectoid

$$
C_{\alpha}=0.022 wt \% C
$$
$$
C_{{Fe_{3}C}}=6.70 wt \% C
$$

*Part II*: Use the lever rule with the tie line shown

$$
W_{Fe_{3}C} = \frac{R}{R+S}=\frac{C_{o}-C_{\alpha}}{C_{Fe_{3}C}-C_{\alpha}}
$$
$$
= \frac{0.40-0.022}{6.70-0.022}=0.057
$$
