# Spheroidite

```ad-summary
If *pearlie* or *bainite* microstructures are heated to a tmeprature below the eutectoid temperature ($727 \degree C$) and keep for a sufficient long time, spheroidite will form.
```

- $\alpha$ grains with spherical $Fe_{3}C$
- Diffusion dependent
- Reduces Interface
- **Soft and ductile**

# Martensite

- Changes from $\gamma$ (FCC) to Martensite (BCT)
- Forms needles within the Austenite
- Rapid transformation as the % transformation depends on $T$ only
- Done via **quenching**
