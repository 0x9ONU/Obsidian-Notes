# Diffusion

```ad-summary
In an alloy, atoms tend to migrate from regions of high concentration to regions of low concentration
```

## Diffusion Mechanisms

**Vacancy**
- Atoms exchange vacancies

**Intersitial**
- Sammler atoms can diffuse between atoms
- *Carbon Steel mechanism* also known as **case hardening**
	- Diffuse carbon atoms into the host iron atoms at the surface.

## Rate of Diffusion

$$
J = \text{Flux} = \frac{\text{moles (or mass) transfer}}{(\text{surface area})(\text{time})} = \frac{mol}{m^2s}
$$

### Steady-State Diffusion

$$
\text{Flux proportional to concentration gradient = dC/dx}
$$
*Fick’s first law of diffusion*
$$
J = -D \frac{dC}{dx}
$$
*if linear*
$$
\frac{dC}{dx} \approx \frac{\Delta C}{\Delta x}
$$
