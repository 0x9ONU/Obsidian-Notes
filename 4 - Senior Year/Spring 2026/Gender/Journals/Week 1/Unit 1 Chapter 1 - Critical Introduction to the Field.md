# Androcentrism

**Androcentrism**: The privileging of male and masculine ways of understanding the world
- Used to be normalized that all knowledge in the world ended up being produced by white men

Feminist scholars argue that this can often obscure the way that we should think about it.
- Interdisciplinary and wide-spread group of people
  
# History

- Women’s Liberation Movement and Civil Rights Movement of the mid-20th Century
	- Called attention to the unfair conditions placed by androcentrism
- In the 1970s, universities started instituting departments to help margninalized groups in response
- Started off with white straight women, and eventually moved towards color, immigrants, LGBTQ, poor, disability, religion, sexuality, etc.
- Dude to their wide range of studies, they are now being renamed to “Women, Gender, and Sexuality Studies” deparments.
