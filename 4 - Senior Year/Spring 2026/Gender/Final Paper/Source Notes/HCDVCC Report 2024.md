
# Domestic Abuse

This study highlights that bisexual women experience some of the highest rates of intimate partner violence (IPV)—**61.1%** compared to 43.8% of lesbian women.
- Modern sources focus on "identity abuse," such as an abuser threatening to "out" a partner to their employer or family or withholding gender-affirming hormones as a means of psychological control.

**Prevalence in Men:** **37.3% of bisexual men** and **26% of gay men** report similar experiences, also exceeding the rates of heterosexual men.

- **Unique Barriers to Help:**
    - **Identity-Based Abuse:** Abusers often use "outing" (threatening to reveal a partner's identity to employers or family) as a primary tactic of control.
    - **Systemic Distrust:** Many survivors fear that reporting abuse will lead to further discrimination from the police or that mainstream shelters will not be "equipped" to handle their specific needs.
