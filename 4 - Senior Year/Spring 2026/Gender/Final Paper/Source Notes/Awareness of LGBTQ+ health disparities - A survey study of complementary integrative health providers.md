The article **"Awareness of LGBTQ+ health disparities: A survey study of complementary integrative health providers"** (2023) examines the level of knowledge that providers like chiropractors, naturopaths, acupuncturists, and massage therapists have regarding the health challenges faced by the LGBTQ+ community.

# Core Findings

- **General Awareness vs. Specific Knowledge**: Most providers (over 70%) agree that LGBTQ+ individuals face general discrimination and health disparities. However, they often lack knowledge of **specific** disparities.
- **Substance Abuse Disparity Gap**: There is a significant lack of awareness regarding higher rates of substance abuse. More than 60% of providers were unaware that LGBTQ+ adults and youth are at a higher risk for alcohol, tobacco, and drug misuse.
- **Mental Health Awareness**: While roughly 60% recognized that transgender and LGBTQ+ individuals have higher rates of suicidal ideation, nearly 30% of providers remained unaware of this critical risk.
- **Provider Identity and Awareness**: Providers who identify as part of the LGBTQ+ community (specifically LGB females in the study) generally showed a significantly higher awareness of these disparities than their heterosexual cisgender counterparts.

# Barriers and Education

- **Lack of Training**: The study found that 39% of providers had received **no cultural competency training** specific to LGBTQ+ issues.
- **Clinical Comfort**: Despite the lack of specific knowledge, most providers (over 80%) reported feeling comfortable with patients disclosing their sexual orientation and confident in their ability to provide care.
- **Educational Recommendations**: The authors conclude that better education is urgently needed in both professional degree programs and through continuing education (conferences, webinars) to help providers recognize and mitigate these preventable health gaps.

# Why This Matters

Health disparities are largely **preventable** when providers recognize them and follow best-practice protocols. Because complementary integrative health providers often see patients for long-term wellness, their awareness is crucial for early intervention and improved health outcomes in the LGBTQ+ community.
