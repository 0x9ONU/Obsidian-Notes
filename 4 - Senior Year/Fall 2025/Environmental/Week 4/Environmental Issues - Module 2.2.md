# Yellowstone Gray Wolf Restoration Project: A Case Study in Population Ecology

Wolves were eradicated in the lower 48 states of the U.S. and were listed as an endangered species in 1974
- Critics feared that without wolves, the population of herbivores would not be kept in check.
- They were reintroduced to Yellowstone in 1995, and the population is stabilizing.

```ad-important
Ecologist study populations to better understand what makes them thrive, decline, or become overpopulated, in an effort to manage them and the other populations they impact.
```

# Ecologists Studying Population

```ad-summary
title: Definition
**A population** is all the individuals of a species that live in the same geographic area and are able to interact and interbreed.
```

*Range*: The geographic area where a species or one of its populations can be found.

# Population Distributions

```ad-important
A population's distribution within its range is influenced by bheavioral and ecological factors and is a reflection of how individuals interact with each other and their environmental.
```

**Population Distribution**: The location and spacing of individuals within their range
- Can be clumped, random, or uniform

*Clumped*: Individuals that around found in groups or patches within the habitat
- Offers protection against predators

![[Pasted image 20250917140111.png | center]]

*Random*: Individuals are spread out over the environment irregularly, with no discernible pattern.
- Wildflowers have their seeds distributed by the winds
- Sometimes seen in homogeneous environements

![[Pasted image 20250917140208.png | center ]]

*Uniform*: Individuals are spaced evenly, perhaps due to territorial behavior or mechanisms.
- Rare in nature
