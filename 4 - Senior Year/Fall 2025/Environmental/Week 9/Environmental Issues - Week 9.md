## Limitations of Mainstream Economics

1. Lack of *true cost accounting* 
	- Internal costs: Trees, Labor, Equipment
	- External costs: Health problems, pollution, 
2. Linear (one-way) systems
	- There is no loop-back from waste (straight from resources to waste and no reclaiming)
3. Discounts Future Use
	- Does not consider resources for the future resources and assumes they are infinite
