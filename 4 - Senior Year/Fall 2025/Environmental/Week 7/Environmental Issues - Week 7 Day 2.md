- This led to the population growth rate declining

## Human Population: Past, Present, and Future

**Population Growth Rate** has been slow for most of human history

*Two Historical Growth Spurts*:
1. Agricultural Revolution (~10,000 years ago)
	- Transformed from hunter-gather society to agrarian societies
	- What do you need?
		- More labor force
		- Producing more food
	- Life expectancy *increased*
	- High birth rates
	- This led to a population boom
2. Industrial Revolution (1700s)
	- Industry became more important and people became more concentrated in cities
	- Increase in sanitation
	- Life expectancy increased as well

**Fast Growth Rate in Modern Times**
- 1800-1930: $1 \to 2$ billion
- 1930-1960: $2 \to 3$ billion
- 1960-1974: $3 \to 4$ billion
- …
- 1999-2011: $6 \to 7$ billion

### The Struggle for Existence

**Thomas Malthus (1766-1834)**
- Thought a lot about human populations 
- Influenced Darwin
- Saw that resources were growing linearly, while human population was growing exponentially, which could lead to a break in carrying capacity
