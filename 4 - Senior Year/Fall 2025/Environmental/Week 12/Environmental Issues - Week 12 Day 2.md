## Riots Over Food?

- **2008**: Prices more than doubled fro rice, corn, and soybeans
	- Led to riots/revolts because people could not feed their families
	- More poorer countries spend a significant amount of their incomes on food alone

It is decreasing, but the percentage is still high
- Around ~1 billion people deal with problems relating to hunger
- We grow enough food to feed everyone, but that many people still starve

### Food Security

```ad-summary
The access to safe and nutritious foods
- Socially
- Financially
- Physically
```

Can be affected by **protracted crisis**:
- War
- Political Issues

![[Pasted image 20251119101820.png]]

### Malnutrition

Poor health from:
1. *Undernutrition*: Not enough calories
2. *Deficiency*: Not having enough of specific nutrient 
	- (ex. Lack of Iodine causes Goyers)
3. *Overnutrition*: Too many calories
	- Can be caused by **food deserts** (places that do not have access to food that has positive nutrition)

## UN Sustainable Development Agenda

**Two Main Goals**:
1. Eliminate poverty
2. Zero hunger

Proposed methods:
- Increasing production of small farms in developing countries
- Address the distribution of food
