# Greenland Vikings 2: Electric Boogaloo

```ad-question
The big question is "why was the settlement unsustainable?"
```

The *five* factors:
1. Natural climate change (for ice cores)
2. Self-Inflicted Environmental damage (from mud cores)
	- Overgrazing
	- Soil-erosion
	- Overharvesting of grass (used as insulation)
	- Harvested a lot of timber as well
3. Failure to respond to the environment (From Garbage Heaps)
	- Lack of fish in their diet, even though they lived on the coast
	- Switched to some marine resources (walrus and seals)
4. Hostile Neighbors
	- Did not adopt Inuit customs
5. Loss of Friendly Neighbors
	- European suppliers stopped arriving

### What Challenges Do WE Face in Dealing with Environmental Issues?

We fall into **social traps**. The short-term benefits that have *long-term* negative consequences
1. *Tragedy of the Commons:* Degrading of a common resource

![[Pasted image 20250903104007.png | center]]

2. *Time Delay*: Benefit today, but it sets into  motion events that cause problems later on

![[Pasted image 20250903104128.png | center]]

3. *Sliding Reinforcer*: Actions beneficial at first, but a change in conditions cause a decline overtime
