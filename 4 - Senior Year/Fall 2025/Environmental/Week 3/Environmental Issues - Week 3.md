# Module 2.1: Ecosystems and Nutrient Cycling

## Ecological Hierarchy: From Biosphere to Individual

### Ecosystem-scale Experiments: A Case Study in Ecosystems and Nutrient Cycling

- FACE (Free-Air CO2 Enrichment) experiences
	- 1970s
	- Tried to find the response between rising CO2 levels and ecosystems
	- They found it was very difficult to recreate in a small-scale environment
	- The ecosysems are very complex
	- They created FACE to combat this
- Manipulating temperature and Co2 levels in outdoor enclosures offer a view of their effect intact ecosystems

#### SPRUCE (Spruce and Peatland Responses Under Changing Environments)

**Peatlands**: Wetlands with poor soil which contain plants adpated to those conditions, like Sphagnum moss
- Due to slow decomposition rates, peatlands are *carbon sinks*: They store a lot of carbon in their soil
- The big question is does peatlands getting warmer due to global warming will release more carbon, which makes global warming even worse
- Used large pods without disturbing the natural area
- Simulated new near-future conditions to find changes
