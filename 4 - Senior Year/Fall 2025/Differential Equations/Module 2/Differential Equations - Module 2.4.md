# Inverse of a Matrix

```ad-summary
title: Definition
Let $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$ are matrices where $\mathbf{AB}=\mathbf{I}=\mathbf{BA}$, then
- $\mathbf{A}$ is invertible
- $\mathbf{B}$ is the inverse of $\mathbf{A}$, denoted by $\mathbf{A}^{-1}$
```
