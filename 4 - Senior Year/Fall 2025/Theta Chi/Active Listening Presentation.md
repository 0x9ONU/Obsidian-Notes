### Reasons We Communicate

To satisfy needs:

- **Identity Needs**: To explore, find, and satisfy esteem needs
- **Social Needs**: Build community for ourselves
- **Practical Needs**: Shit needs done

### Components of Listening

Hearing:
- The physical process
- Always happening
- Can take in everything (Noise including)

Attending:
- Taking the noise out, but not fully understanding what is being said

Understanding:
- Decode the message to make sense of it
- Can hear/attend without understanding
- Affected by knowledge of a topic or language

Interpreting
- Similar to understanding
- BUT more than just decoding
- Takes contextual variables to provide additional information

Remembering
- Recalling the message sen
- You remember
	- 50% of a message right after hearing it
	- 35% within 8 hours
	- 25% after 2 months
- Affected by:
	- Reptition
	- Amount of info
	- Ability to rehearse

# Process of Active Listening

1. Silent Listening
	- Shut up and respond non-verbally to show you are listening
2. Questioning
	- Ask questions to clarify, learn feelings, encourage elaboration, and gather more facts and details
3. Paraphrasing
	- Repeat what you think you heard in your own words
4. Empathizing
	- Shows that the listener identifies with the speaker
	- Don’t deny other the right to their feelings
5. Supporting
	- Shows solidarity with the other person
	- Agreements
	- Offers to help
	- 
6. Analyzing
	- Offer a different POV
7. Evaluating
	- Appraises the speaker’s thoughts and behaviors either favorable
8. Advising
	- Offer an opinion to sovle the problem
	- Be sure they want a solution
	- Are ready to hear a solution
	- Ready to accept the advice
	- ONLY GIVE ADVICE YOU WOULD ALSO TAKE

## Bad Habits of Listening

Interrupting
- Stopping the speaker from finishing their thought
- Cues might have been misread

Eavesdropping
- Planned attempt to secretly listen
- Can damage relationships

Aggressive listening
- Listening to ambush speaker

Narcissistic listening
- AKA stage hogging
- Self-centered listening
- Brings attention back onto oneself
- Can be unintentional or intentional
	- An attempt gone wrong at Paraphrasing or empathizing would be unintentional

Selective Listening
- Choosing what to listen to and not acknowledge the whole message

Listening Overload
- Getting too much info too fast
- Not acknowledging and working to manage this bad habit
- Ways to maage this
	- take notes
	- Ask questions
	- Ask for them to slow down or repeat things

Skipping to Advising
- Not taking the time to clearly understand the situation or go through the other stages of the process

Never being critical in the evaluation stage
- Always telling people they are right regardless of their actions
