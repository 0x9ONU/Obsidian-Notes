## Fragment 11.1

- First part speaks of his spirit being taken by a goddess to the gates of Night and Day
- There, he was let into the gate since he took the path away from evil and the typical path humans take
	- He took the path of **right** and **justice**
- There is a perfect persuasive **Truth** which deities possess
- Mortals have opinions
```ad-important
"...that it is right that the objects of
opinion genuinely are, being always, indeed, all things."
```

- There is a way such that opinions can become truth for objects in the real world.
## Fragment 11.2

- The path of the **Peruasion** accompanies Truth
	- Both “is” and “it is not the case that it is not”
- The other paths are not right.
	- “is not” and that “is not”
- You can not know what is not, nor can you declare it

## Fragment 11.3

- Things people can think of may also be absolute Truth
- Implies that humans can think in Truths

## Fragment 11.4

- Objects are neither scattered everywhere at once nor have a force to be brought back together
## Fragment 11.5

- Things will eventually come back to where they started (circular motion)

## Fragment 11.6

- There is no such thing that you should not thing about
- Humans must always investigate their world and find explanations for everything
- If not, they are left helpless and their knowledge will turn backwards if no effort is taken

## Fragment 11.7

- Use judgement and reason rather than that of told mythos to explain the world around you
- Do not let mythos hold you back from investigating the world

## Fragment 11.8

- There is only one true truth that Justice enforces
- Things that are gone/in the past cannot be investigated

```ad-important
"What is to be thought of is the same as that on account of which the thought is."
```

- “What-is” is limitless and complete in all directions like a sphere. Aka there is universal truth.
