### *Categories*

In Aristotle’s early lectures, he introduced his disciples to how substances are defined and sorted. For the sake of understanding the soul, first the types of subjects must be defined and their implications must be defined to further bring upon his understanding of the soul. He defines all substances into four different categories based on two binary relationships called “said of a subject” and “present in a subject.” The first relationship is used to define when a substance has a subject-predicate relationship to another, while the second relationship defines a relationship that exists dependently or independently. When something is “said of a subject,” it gives an object specificity (Aristotle) and becomes general when it is absent (man). When something is “present in a subject”, it defines a characteristic that cannot exist without another substance. For example, the color of a dog cannot exist without the dog existing. 

From there, Aristotle further defines the difference between a primary substance and a secondary substance. Both category is a subcategory of a specific substance that creates a hierarchy between a specific individual (Aristole) and a group of specific individuals (Men as a species). Aristotle makes it clear that primary substances are special as they contain the “this” (*Tode ti*) of a substance and are the purest form of a substance. Other qualities include not having any contrary and does not admit of any degree. That means that they cannot be contrast (such as the qualities of hot or cold) and ensures that a primary substance is the same as a secondary substance other than it being more specific. 
### *On the Soul*

With this knowledge of substances, the next crucial to go into depth about his theory on both the mind and psychology in his *on the soul* lectures excerpts. In the second book of his lectures, he defines three types of substances. The first type, which is called the Form or *Tode ti*, is an actuality that defines a substance’s specificity. The second type, called Matter, is what a substance is made out of at a general level, which acts as a potentiality since it can become a specific kind of thing.
