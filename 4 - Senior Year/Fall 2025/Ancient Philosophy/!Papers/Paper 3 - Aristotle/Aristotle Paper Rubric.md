Grading:  
No Title or no label, “Abstract”: 2-point penalty each.  
Abstract: 10 points  
- Clearly stated thesis statement.  
- Clear summary.  
- Within the word limit: 3-point penalty.  
Exposition: 30 points  
- Demonstration of the understanding of the relevant arguments of Aristotle (it must be the class  
version, using the power-point/notes posted on Canvas)  
Analysis: 30 points  
- Objection [clearly stated and relevant] and Example [appropriate]  
- Response [effectively addressed and consistent] and Example [appropriate]  
- Defense [Effective and consistent with Abstract]  
Failure to write the minimum word limit of each section, 4 points deduction per section.  
Anywhere between 2 – 4 points deduction for a “filler” and unnecessary repetition for the sake of  
meeting the word limit.  
Late submission: 4 points < 24 hours; otherwise, 7 points deduction; >48 hours, the paper will not be  
accepted and you will get 0.
