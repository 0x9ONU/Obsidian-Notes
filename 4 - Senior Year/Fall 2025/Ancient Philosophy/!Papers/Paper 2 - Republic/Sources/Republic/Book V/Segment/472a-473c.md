Do you think that someone is a worse painter if, having painted a model of what the finest and most beautiful human being would be like and having rendered every detail of his picture adequately, he could not prove that such a man could come into being? There is one change we could point to that, in my opinion, would accomplish this. It’s certainly neither small nor easy, but it is possible. What is it? Well, I’ve now come to what we likened to the greatest wave. But I shall say what I have to say, even if the wave is a wave of laughter that will simply drown me in ridicule and contempt.
