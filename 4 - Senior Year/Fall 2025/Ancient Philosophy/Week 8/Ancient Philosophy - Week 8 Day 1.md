**Three Cognitive States to Each Realm**:
1. Ignorance is of nothing (what is not), which is completely unknowable
2. An opinion (which is a power) is an intermediate between knowledge and ignorance, which is concerned with what is and what is not. EX. take any beautiful thing, and it will appear ugly in a certain way and many participate in both opposites.
3. Knowledge (which is also a power) is of what is, which is completely knowable.
