# Republic IV: Justice Defined in Both the City-State and the Individual

## Four Cardinal Virtues

Three main parts;
1. Rulers
2. Auxiliaries
3. Producers

If the ideal city is good, then it is:
1. Wise
2. Courageous
3. Moderate
4. Just

```ad-note
Wisdom, Courage, and Moderation are defined first. THen whatever is "leftover" must be Justice
```

### Wisdom

A wise city exhibits good judgment based on *knowledge*

```ad-note
There aer a wide varieties of knowledge (ex. the one posessed by the carpenters, the farmers, etc.), but the relevant one is the knowledge posssed by the *rulers* (although they are few in number).
```

### Courage

It is exhibited by the *auxiliaries*, the part of the city that protect the city.
- 429b-c: Courage is defined as “the power to preserve one’s belief about what things are to be feared”
- 429d-430b: The law dictates what is to be feared; the proper nature and upbringing (ex. proper education) produce the right habit and belief.
- 430c: He specifies *civic courage* is being described. They do it out of **habit**, rather than of knowledge. Their character will not be changed regardless of what happens. The knowledge is passed down by rulers.

```ad-quote
title: 429d-430b
You know that dyers, who want to dye wool purple, first pick out from the many colors ofwool the one that is naturally white, then they carefully prepare this in various ways, so that it will absorb the color as well as possible, and only at that point do they apply the purple dye. When something is dyed in this way, the color is fast—no amount of washing, e whether with soap or without it, can remove it. But you also know what happens to material if it hasn’t been dyed in this way, but instead is dyed purple or some other color without careful preparation. What we were contriving was nothing other 430 than this: That because they had the proper nature and upbringing, they would absorb the laws in the finest possible way, just like a dye, so that their belief about what they should fear and all the rest would become so fast that even such extremely effective detergents as pleasure, pain, fear, and desire wouldn’t wash it out—and pleasure is much more potent than any powder, washing soda, or soap. This power to preserve through b everything the correct and law-inculcated belief about what is to be feared and what isn’t is what I call courage, unless, of course, you say otherwise
```

```ad-important
The reuslt is that they will preserve despite any counter pleasure, pain, fear, and desire.
```

### Moderation

It is a kind of order and the mastery of certain kinds of pleasures and desires
- Often it means “self-control”: *the better part rules the worst part.*
- This occurs when the rulers and the ruled both share the same belief who should rule.
- Moderation spreads throughout the whole and occurs in every part of the city; but it is most dominant virtue of the ruled.

### Justice

```ad-summary
title: Definition
The power that makes it possible for each of the class to do one's ownt ask. It is doing one's work and the power to do so.
```
