# Ionian Philosophers

## Xenophanes

- A poet from Colophon
- Flourished around 570 BC.
- Made explicit what was implicit in Milesian philosophers: **no room left for *anthropomorphic gods***

```ad-note
- Thales: All things are full of gods
- Anaximander's *Apeiron* posssesses the attributes of god (eternal and ageless)
- Anaximenes's air is closely linked to breathe and life (or the soul)
```
