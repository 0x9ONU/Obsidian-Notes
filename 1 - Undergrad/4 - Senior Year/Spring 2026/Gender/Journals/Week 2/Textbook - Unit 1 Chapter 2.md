# Theorizing Lived Experiences

```ad-summary
title: Definition
**Socially-lived Thoerizing**: Creating feminist theories and knowledge from the actual day-to-day experiences of groups of people who have raditionally been excluded from the production of academic knowledge.
```

Able to show how personal experiences are shaped by:
- politics
- economics
- culture

By understanding people from the “bottom” of the social system, we can have a more complete knowledge of he current systems of power
- Different points of view can create different theories about society

## Feminism

```ad-important
Based on the belief that knowledge is shaped by the political and social context in which it is made.
```

A set of diverse theories and analytical perspectives:
- Socialist theories
- Radical sex theories
- Black theories
- Queer theories
- Transfeminist theories
- Disability theories
- Intersectional theories.

*Reflexivity*: understanding how one’s social position influences the ways they understand the world.
- Combination of identities shape a person's social experience.

### Clash Between the “American Dream” and Feminism

US individuals are taught to think that people are self-activating and self-actualizing individuals.

However, feminism believes that *agency* alone cannot determine an individual’s outcome
- Can be limited or enhanced by their social position.

Experiences can be broken down into *four levels of analysis*:
1. **Micro (self)**: what an individual lives through everyday
2. **Meso (group)**: focuses on how communities and organizations structure social life.
3. **Macro (structural)**: Consists of government policies, programs, institutions, and ideologies
4. **Global**: Transnational production, trade, migration, global capitalism, trade and law bodies.
