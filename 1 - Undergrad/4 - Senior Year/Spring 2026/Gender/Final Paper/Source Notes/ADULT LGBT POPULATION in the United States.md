# Substance Abuse

Research comparing transgender and cisgender adults shows that **transgender nonbinary (TGNB) adults** report the highest rates of hazardous drinking and problematic drug use compared to other groups within the community.

```ad-warning
Interestingly, while transgender and cisgender adults reported **similar rates of hazardous drinking** (approx. 28%), transgender adults faced drastically higher mental health burdens.
```

# Depression

- **Suicidality Gap:**
    - **81%** of transgender adults have thought about suicide in their lifetime, compared to **35%** of cisgender adults.
    - **42%** of transgender adults have attempted suicide, nearly four times the rate of cisgender adults (**11%**).

**Non-Suicidal Self-Injury (NSSI):** Over half (**56%**) of transgender adults reported engaging in self-injury, compared to only **12%** of cisgender adults.
**Highest Risk Group:** Transgender **nonbinary** adults consistently reported the highest rates of both substance use and poor mental health within the community.
