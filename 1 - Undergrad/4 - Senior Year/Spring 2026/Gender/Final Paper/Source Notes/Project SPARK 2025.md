# Substance Abuse

This study found that **54% of LGBTQ+ youth** currently use at least one substance (alcohol, marijuana, or tobacco). Notably, it highlighted that **discrimination and victimization** are primary drivers, with youth who experienced identity-based discrimination reporting much higher rates of use (60%) than those who did not (51%).

# Depression

This report observed that from 2023 to 2025, the percentage of LGBTQ+ youth reporting depressive symptoms rose from **48% to 54%**.
- The data shows a direct link between the inability to meet **basic needs** (housing, food) and higher odds of developing depression, suggesting that mental health in the community is as much an economic issue as it is a social one.

**Worsening Mental Health Trends:**
- **Anxiety:** Reported symptoms increased from **57% to 68%** over one year.
- **Depression:** Reported symptoms rose from **48% to 54%**.
- **Suicidal Ideation:** The percentage of youth reporting suicidal thoughts grew from **41% to 47%**.

**Identity-Specific Disparities:**

- **TGNB Youth:** Transgender and nonbinary (TGNB) youth reported significantly higher rates of depression (**58%**) and suicidal ideation (**53%**) compared to cisgender peers (**36%** and **28%**, respectively).
- **Conversion Therapy:** A concerning trend was the doubling of reports of _threats_ of conversion therapy (from **11% to 22%**) and an increase in actual _exposure_ to the practice (from **9% to 15%**)

```ad-important
Despite worsening symptoms, **help-seeking behavior doubled**. The proportion of youth reaching out to mental health professionals during crises rose from **32% to 64%**, suggesting reduced stigma around seeking support.
```

# Religion

Interestingly, for youth who find **affirming** religious spaces, the importance of spirituality is actually associated with _lower_ rates of depression (55% vs 58%), suggesting that the conflict is not with faith itself, but with non-affirming religious environments.
