# Question 2

## Car Suspension

You want to make sure a car suspension is rigid as a spring. Low carbon steel could lead to it being bending and permanently deformed. It would eventually snap and fracture in the wheel well.

## Crankshaft

High carbon steel in a crank shaft could not be ductile enough and could snap under load.

## Blades

Low carbon steel blades would deform over time, loose its sharpness, and not be able to cut through materials anymore

## Car Body

High carbon steel could not be molded and would shatter rather than deform during a crash. 

## Manhole Cover

A low carbon steel manhole cover could bend over time, which could lead to it breaking overtime and cause injury

## Pipes

Ductile cast iron pipes under pressure could be too brittle and would snap under heavy pressures rather than deforming

## Building

A building using high carbon steel is more likely to crack rather than deform.
