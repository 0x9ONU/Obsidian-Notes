# Lab Answers

Plastics and Microplastics ( Station 1)

1. They come from resin pellets and beads. Microbeads come from personal items like toothpaste, cosmetics, face washes, etc.
2. Small plastic pieces, usually less than 5mm in size, come in many different forms.
3. They can end up in sludge and treated wastewater. 
4. This statement is accurate. According to the LBM(cited below), microplastics are located in various centers in the human body, such as the liver, spleen, heart, lungs, thymus, blood, reproductive organs, kidneys, and even the brain [1, 3].
5. Scientists don’t have a full grasp on the effects of plastics on the body. However, some studies have shown that microplastics can cause irreversible DNA damage [2].

Addressing PFAS (Station 2)

1. Perfluoroalkyl and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS) [4]
2. Manufacturing chemicals for consumer products since the 1940s
3. Found in the blood of people and animals all over the world, it may be linked to harmful effects like fertility issues, natural levels of hormones, increased risk of cancer, and differing delays of effect in children in development.  [5-6]
4. Roughly the 1940s (85 years)
5. Companies are currently being required to release an inventory report for additional PFAS strains, and increased methods of testing the overall levels of PFAS located within local bodies of water. In addition, the EPA also requires that all drinking water be enforced to have less than 4 parts-per-trillion (aka ng/L) [7-8]
6. A study conducted by the FDA shows that the U.S. food supply is very well regulated and only about 3% of all documented food has been shown to contain a minimal amount of PFAS. However, a study from 2023 shows that vegetables have a higher rate of PFAS over the attention rate set by the European commission for food safety [10-11].

Renewable Energy (Station 3)

1. For the title of an energy to be deemed as “renewable, the following criteria must be met:
	- Derived from a natural source [12]
	- Replenished at a higher rate than it is consumed [12]

3. The total amount of energy consumed by the US by renewable energy is 13%, with a projection of a significant increase within the next 20 years [13].
