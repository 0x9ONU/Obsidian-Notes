## The Environment

```ad-summary
title: Definition
Biological and physical surroundings in which any given living organism exists.
```

**Environmental Science**: Field of research to understand the natural world and our relationship to it.
- It is *highly interdisciplinary*

```ad-example
- Natural sciences
	- What is the climate like?
	- How can soil erosiion be prevented?
- Social sciences
	- How have indigeous people lived here?
	- What enivornmental policies would best fit the culture or place?
- Humanities
```

### Different Scientific Approaches Have Different Goals and Outcomes


| Emperical Science                                                     | Applied Science                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Systematic observation and experimentation to study the natural world | Address a problem or need for a cause.         |
| Used to investigate the natural world                                 | Knowledge is used to address problems or needs |
### What Characteristics Make an Environmental Dilemma a “Wicked Problem”?

```ad-warning
Multifaceted, complex and difficult to solve
```

```ad-example
**Climate Change**
- Multiple causes (fossil fuels, methane, etc.)
- Multiople consequences (rising sea levels)
- SOlutions with trade-offs (types of green energy)
```

**Solutions to these Problems**:
1. *Environmental Literacy*: Basic understanding of how ecosystems function and of the impact of our choices on the environment
2. *Trade-Offs*: There is no perfect solution
3. *Triple Bottom Line*: We have to be aware of the environmental, social, and economic consequences
