## Cycles: What we Need to Know for the Exam

```ad-question
1. Where is the nutrients stored?
2. How are they moved between the storage areas?
```

Example: Carbon is stored in 
1. All living organisms
2. Soil
3. Air

How does it move:
1. Photosynthesis (Producers take carbon out of the air into sugars with the help of the sun)
2. Cellular Respiration: (Producers and consumers break down sugars into energy, and release carbon into the air)

How does nitrogen move?
1. Bacteria can nitrafy and denitrify the soil
2. Returned from bones through decomposes and bacteria
3. Turned into Ammonia from the atmosphere into Nitrates that plants can use
4. Can be put back into the atmosphere via bacteria

## Keystone Organisms

Ecosystems need specific organisms to have a particular role, which has a disproportional effect on the environment

```ad-example
- **Top Down**: Wolves in Yellowstone kept the elk populations in check to make sure that overgrazing did not happen
- **Bottom Up**: Plants did not grow properly, which led to poor elk populations and wolf population decline
```
