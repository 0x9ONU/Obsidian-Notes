### Her Work Against Pesticides

- Used a chemical called DDT to kill insects on everything! (crops, humans, etc.)
- She saw that DDT is a cause for a decline in a bird population
- Found that DDT was a prevalent *carcinogen* 
- “The Great Cranberry Scandal”
	- Found that most of the country’s cranberries were contaminated with DDT right before Thanksgiving

### Silent Spring

Silent Spring began a grassroots effort to regulate harmful chemicals
- Carson accused chemical companies of spreading misinformation
- Testified before JFK’s Science Advisory Committee
- DDT was banned in 1972

### Her Legacy: There is More Work to Do
