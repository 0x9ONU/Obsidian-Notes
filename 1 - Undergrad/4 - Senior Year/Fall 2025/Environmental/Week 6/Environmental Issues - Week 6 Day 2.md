## The Pace of Evolution

It is generally slow, but a variety of factors speed/slow it down


| Speeds Up                      | Slows Down      |
| ------------------------------ | --------------- |
| Genetic Diversity              | Population Size |
| Strength of Selective Pressure | Generation Time |

## The Pace of Extinction

Also, generally slow

*Background Extinction Rate*: There is always a few species go extinct every year

![[Pasted image 20251001100749.png]]

**Mass Extinction**: Where >75% species die in a short time
- Usually caused by major atmospheric shifts
- It opens up niches for other species

## Case Study: What is Happening to the Birds on the Island of Guam

There are a ton of *Endemic* birds on Guam that were adapted to specific niches
- Species that are native to an area and are restricted geographically

```ad-info
title: Hypotheses
They thought the following three could be the cause of the mass extinction:
- Pesticidies
- Disease
- **Brown Tree Snake (Invasive Species)**
```

*Brown Tree Snake*: A snake that can live both in the ground and in trees
- Accidentally introduced in the 1950s.
- Provided the only predator that was like this in their habitat
- Birds would not even fly away from these snakes because they did not know they were a threat

*Current Efforts*
- “Mouse Bombs” laced with acetaminophen to poison the smaller snakes

# Biodiversity

## Case Study: Palm Oil Plantations in SE Asia

Large fruits come from palm trees and can be used for cooking oil
- It makes up around 50% of oil
- 48% comes form Indonesia and 39% comes from Malaysia

Why do we use Palm Oil?
- Replacement for trans fats
- Trans fats, with health concerns, were banned in the USA under normal circumstances
- Most productive oil-bearing crop
- Cheaper than other oils
- Widely used in packaged food and household products
