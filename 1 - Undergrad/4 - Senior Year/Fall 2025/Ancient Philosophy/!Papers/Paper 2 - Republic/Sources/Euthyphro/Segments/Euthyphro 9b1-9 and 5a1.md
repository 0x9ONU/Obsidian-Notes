# Original Text

```ad-quote
Euthyphro: This is perhaps no light task, Socrates, though I could
show you very clearly.
Socrates: I understand that you think me more dull-witted than
the jury, as you will obviously show them that these actions were unjust
and that all the gods hate such actions.
Euthyphro: I will show it to them clearly, Socrates, if only they will
listen to me.

Euthyphro: I should be of no use, Socrates, and Euthyphro would
not be superior to the majority of men, if I did not have accurate
knowledge of all such things.
```
