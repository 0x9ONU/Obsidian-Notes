# Original Text

```ad-quote
Do you think, then, that there’s any difference between the blind and those who are really deprived ofthe knowledge of each thing that is? The latter have no clear model in their souls, and so they cannot—in the manner of painters—look to what is most true, make constant reference d to it, and study it as exactly as possible. Hence they cannot establish here on earth conventions about what is fine or just or good, when they need to be established, or guard and preserve them, once they have been established
```
