# Original Text

```ad-quote
That which is there to be spoken and thought of must be. For it is possible for it to be, but not possible for nothing to be. I bid you consider this. For I bar your way from this first way of inquiry, but next form the way on which mortals, knowing nothing, two-headed, wander. FOr helplessness in their breasts guides their wandering mind. But they are carried on equally deaf and blind, amazed, hordes without judgement by whom it (namely, what-is) is thought both to be and not to be the same
and not the same; but the path of all is backward-turning.
```

# Analysis

#### Argument

```ad-important
What can be said and be thought of must be; for it can be, and nothing cannot
```

- It is possible for “what can be said and be thought of” to be.
- It is not possible for nothing to be.
- “What can be said and be thought of” is not nothing
- “What can be said and be thought of” is something.
- “What can be said and be thought of” exists.

Nothing cannot exit. It is necessary true that what does not exist doe snot exist.
