
During my time in Theta Chi at Ohio Northern University, I have spent a considerable amount of time, effort, and love into making our chapter the best it could possibly be. As a freshman, I was elected to be both the Historian and the Fundraising Chair for the chapter. During my term, I was able to raise $500 for our fraternity in the first semester. I would also improve the state of our history by taking upwards of a thousand photographs of our events and would help our chapter through the growing process of going through a new composite company. The following Spring semester, I was assigned to be the Alumni Chairman due to a vacancy in the position. For the first time in 5 years, I was able to put our chapter back into alumni programming across the campus. I ensured that we participated in the banner competition, the parade, the alumni tabling, and also held a tailgate to improve our alumni relations. Afterwards, seeing my contributions, I was elected Vice President and Co-Social Chairman of the chapter as a Sophomore. During my time as Vice President, I completely overhauled our committee system and how committees run. I ensured that brothers would be placed according to their strengths and preferences, while balancing the committees to maximize productivity. I would also introduce a standard meeting minute and transition process to ensure nothing would be lost in translation from year-to-year. Under my leadership, we were able to host a myriad of social, service, and philanthropy events for the campus and the community. Coming from my term of Vice President, I would then get elected to be President of our beloved fraternity. I had large ambitions for my presidency and would do everything in my power to ensure that it would happen. In particular, I wanted to improve our chapter’s GPA, improve communication across the chapter, and ensure that all of our history and artifacts were up to standard. Inspired by the School of Fraternity Practices at the 168th Convention, we started the process of recording all of our artifacts up to museum standard and begun to repair those who were in poor condition. As for our GPA, I would ensure that all brothers were following the scholarship plan, where we saw an improvement from a 3.15 to a 3.40 cumulative average over the year. However, not everything went smoothly, which became a great lesson for my leadership in general. We had a serious scenario came up which put threat to our fraternity. I had to do everything in my power to ensure that our fraternity would not shatter under the pressure, and we came through healthier than ever. Finally, after I retired from my presidency, I would take on both the Formals Chairman and the Greek Week Chairman roles. Between these two roles, I have remained busy up through my last semester of Undergraduate planning both our Greek Sing and our Formals event.
