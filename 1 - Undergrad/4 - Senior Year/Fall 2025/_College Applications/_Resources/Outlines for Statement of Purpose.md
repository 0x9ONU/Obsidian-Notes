
1.     Introduction (first paragraph)
	- Introduce yourself
	- What motivated/inspired you to be an EE/CS/CpE engineer

2.     Educational and Professional Background  (second paragraph)
	- Focus on the key things that motivated you to join research during your undergrad program.
	- Why/how did you select your current topic of research? What made you interested in this topic?
	- How did you equip yourself with the necessary skills required for you research topic, for example taking courses, got trained by another students, learning online, literature review, …, etc.
	- Mention at what extend you received help from other students or advisor to finish tasks. Elaborate in details your contributions including, the problems that you faced, how did you approach these problem, how did you find the solution, at what extend you received help to solve the problem, … etc.
	- At the end you may talk about other professional background like Leadership, teamwork skill, etc as those skills are also important for graduate research.     

3.    Why This Program  (third paragraph)
	- What makes our program stood out. This could be the labs (facilities) they have, the course they offer, the research records in your area, …. etc.

4.    Career Vision  (fourth paragraph)
	- Think about the impact of your research. Future impact on the society.  
	- Short- and long-term professional aspirations.

5.    Why You Are a Good Fit  (fifth paragraph)
	- This could be personal qualities and experiences. You may elaborate on the silks that you have.

6. Conclusion  (sixth paragraph)
	- Strong statement that wraps up the application.
	- Reaffirm enthusiasm and commitment.
